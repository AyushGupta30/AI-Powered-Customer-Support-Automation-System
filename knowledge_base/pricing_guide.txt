PRICING GUIDE
ABC Technologies Cloud Business Management Software

1. STARTER PLAN
Price: $29 per user / month (billed monthly) or $290 per user / year (billed annually,
2 months free).
Includes: Core business management features, up to 5 users, 10GB storage, email support.
Best for: Small businesses and startups.

2. PROFESSIONAL PLAN
Price: $59 per user / month (billed monthly) or $590 per user / year (billed annually,
2 months free).
Includes: All Starter features, unlimited users, 100GB storage, advanced reporting,
priority chat support, API access.
Best for: Growing businesses that need more collaboration tools.

3. ENTERPRISE PLAN
Price: Custom pricing based on organization size and requirements.
Includes: All Professional features, unlimited storage, dedicated account manager,
24/7 priority phone support, custom integrations, single sign-on (SSO), and a
99.9% uptime SLA.
Best for: Large organizations with complex requirements.

4. ADD-ONS
- Advanced Analytics Module: $15 per user / month
- Extra Storage (100GB block): $10 / month
- Premium Onboarding & Training: $499 one-time fee

5. BILLING CYCLES
All plans can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing offers a discount equivalent
to 2 free months compared to monthly billing.

6. FREE TRIAL
All new customers receive a 14-day free trial of the Professional Plan with no credit
card required. No charges apply unless the customer upgrades to a paid plan.

7. DISCOUNTS
- Non-profit organizations: 20% discount on all plans.
- Educational institutions: 30% discount on all plans.
- Annual prepayment: equivalent of 2 months free (as noted above).
