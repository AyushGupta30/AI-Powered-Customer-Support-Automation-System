COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT
ABC Technologies

1. REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase if they are not satisfied with
the product. Refunds for annual subscriptions are processed on a pro-rata basis after
deducting the number of months already used. All refund requests must be approved by
a human supervisor before being processed. Refunds are credited back to the original
payment method within 7-10 business days after approval.

2. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers can cancel their subscription at any time from their account settings.
Cancellation requests submitted through support tickets must be reviewed and approved
by a human supervisor. Once approved, the subscription remains active until the end of
the current billing cycle, after which it will not renew.

3. ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY
Account closure requests are permanent and cannot be undone after 30 days. All account
closure requests must be approved by a human supervisor. Customer data is retained for
90 days after closure in case the customer wishes to reactivate, after which it is
permanently deleted in line with our data retention policy.

4. COMPENSATION POLICY
Customers affected by service outages or major bugs may be eligible for service credit
compensation. All compensation requests must be reviewed and approved by a human
supervisor before being granted, regardless of the amount involved.

5. ESCALATION POLICY
Requests that cannot be resolved by the standard support workflow, or where the
customer explicitly asks to speak to a manager, must be escalated to management.
All escalations require human supervisor approval before a final response is sent.

6. DATA PRIVACY
ABC Technologies does not share customer data with third parties without consent.
All customer information is encrypted at rest and in transit.

7. SUPPORT HOURS
Standard support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Priority support for
Enterprise customers is available 24/7.
