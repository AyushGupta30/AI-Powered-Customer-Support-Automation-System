FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
ABC Technologies

Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
A: Go to Account Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Changes take effect at the start of
the next billing cycle. Downgrades may result in loss of access to features not
included in the lower plan.

Q: Can I get an invoice for my payment?
A: Yes, invoices are automatically emailed after every payment and are also available
under Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History.

Q: How do I add or remove users from my account?
A: Account administrators can manage users under Account Settings > Team Management.
Removing a user immediately revokes their access.

Q: What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?
A: Your data remains accessible in read-only mode for 30 days after cancellation. After
30 days, data is archived, and after 90 days it is permanently deleted per our data
retention policy.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your
registered email address.

Q: Do you offer a mobile app?
A: Yes, ABC Technologies offers mobile apps for both iOS and Android with core
functionality. Some advanced reporting features are only available on desktop/web.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Support can be reached via live chat, email at support@abctechnologies.com, or phone
for Enterprise customers. Standard support hours are Monday-Friday, 9 AM-6 PM.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes, all payment processing is handled through PCI-DSS compliant payment processors,
and ABC Technologies does not store raw credit card numbers on its own servers.
